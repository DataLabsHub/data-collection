Can also be organized by training stage
When used for model training, data can be organized by pre-training, instruction tuning, preference and process supervision, retrieval augmentation and evaluation, with source descriptions attached. Whether a given source can be used for training is determined by that source's terms of use.
Data cleanliness
Finished data is deduplicated and organized into stable fields. Video keeps subtitles the platform already provides and aligns them with the picture, across more than one hundred languages. When the source file has no subtitles, none are created or translated. Pages collected ad hoc by URL retain the content captured at that time.
Audio and video specifications
Resolutions run from 720p to 4K across more than twenty sites. Daily download capacity exceeds 5 PB. Results include picture, sound, comments and metadata.
Images and text
Images cover portraits, street scenes, text recognition and object detection. Text includes news, scholarship, education, healthcare, finance and general pre-training material.
Delivery pipeline
Finished data can be delivered by API, webhook or object storage, including S3, OSS and COS, as JSON, CSV or XLSX. Pulls are partitioned and support incremental updates. Ad hoc collection can also be handed to an agent through MCP and skill packages.
Network coverage
Routes cover more than 200 countries and regions and can target a city. More than 100 million public network resources are available.
Compliance and security
The security assessment meets classified protection level 2. Quality, information security and privacy management correspond to ISO 9001, ISO 27001 and ISO 27701.

02Sources

The existing catalog covers six categories

E-commerce

Products and reviews

Titles, prices, categories, sellers, inventory, reviews. Amazon, eBay and Walmart are ready, and eBay also includes auction records and shipping details.

Social

Conversations and audio/video

Posts, comments, plays and interactions, plus existing subtitles aligned with the footage. Covers Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn company pages and member profiles, and X profile pages.

Search

Queries and results

Queries and results are returned in pairs, including organic results, ads, images, maps, news, scholarship, shopping and hotels, and can be targeted by country, city, language or device. Engines include Google, Bing, Yandex and DuckDuckGo. Average response time is under 1 second, and results are JSON or HTML.

Companies and industries

Vertical text

Funding, revenue, founders, announcements, plus industry text in healthcare, law, recruitment and more. Stock prices and tick-by-tick trade data are not provided.

Code repositories

Source code and collaboration

Source code, full Git history, commits and files, plus pull requests, issues and comments. Public GitHub repositories are ready, with license, languages, stars and activity.

Property

Listings and valuations

Listings, rent, valuations, floor area, year built and nearby schools. Zillow is ready.
