When used for model training, data can be organized by pre-training, fine-tuning and evaluation, with source descriptions attached. Whether a given source can be used for training is determined by that source's terms of use.
Data cleanliness
Finished data is deduplicated and organized into stable fields. Video is time-aligned with subtitles and transcripts, and subtitles cover more than one hundred languages. Pages collected ad hoc by URL retain the content captured at that time.
Delivery pipeline
Finished data is written to object storage, pulled by partition and supports incremental updates. Ad hoc supplementary collection is completed via API.

02Sources

The existing catalog covers five categories

E-commerce

Products and reviews

Titles, prices, categories, sellers, inventory, reviews. Amazon, eBay and Walmart are ready.

Social

Conversations and audio/video

Posts, comments, plays and interactions, plus subtitles aligned with the footage. Covers Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, and the profile pages of LinkedIn and X.

Search

Queries and results

Queries and result pages are returned in pairs and can be targeted by country or city. Engines include Google, Bing, Yandex and DuckDuckGo. Average response time is under 1 second.

Companies and industries

Vertical text

Funding, revenue, founders, announcements, plus industry text in healthcare, law, recruitment and more. Stock prices and tick-by-tick trade data are not provided.

Code repositories

Source code and collaboration

Source code, full Git history, commits and files, plus pull requests, issues and comments. Public GitHub repositories are ready, with license, languages, stars and activity.
